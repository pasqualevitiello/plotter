# Illustration library: decisions

Decided on 2026-11-01.

## Browsing: one ordered collection

The illustrations page shows one list: eight varied drawings first (GEO-01, INT-04, SRV-04, TIM-03, INT-09, MEC-01, CRF-03, TYP-03), then every other drawing by family and code.

- The 16-category filter was dropped. It mixed product area, subject, and drawing method, and only two of its categories matched a job someone arrives with.
- A seven-category taxonomy, then sections (Selected, Empty states, Agents, More) with search, were tried and rejected. With an intentional order, the extra navigation was not needed.
- The `category` field now names a drawing’s code family and sets its place in the order. It is not shown as a filter or caption. Old `?category=` and `?q=` links show the full page.

## Retired near-duplicates

Five drawings were removed from the catalogue (100 → 95). Their sources and codes stay reserved. `retiredStudies` in `components/plotter/illustrations.tsx` maps each one to its replacement, so old `?variant=` links still land on a drawing.

| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| TM02 Room around an appointment | TIM-06 Appointment buffers                     | Same subject; TIM-06 was liked when added       |
| S15 No matching results         | EMP-06 A search, unanswered                    | Same subject; EMP-06 chosen by the user         |
| SW03 A history comes together   | SRV-06 GitHub · A change, brought together     | SRV-06 is an approved reference and adds review |
| SW02 A shorter return           | SRV-01 Cloudflare · Between visitor and origin | SRV-01 is approved and shows hit and miss       |
| AR03 The last piece of the arch | EMP-04 An unbuilt arch                         | EMP-04 is approved and works as a 404           |

These pairs were reviewed side by side and kept as distinct drawings: TIM-01 and TIM-05 (both approved), GEO-03 and INT-10, INT-03 and INT-05, and GEO-08, GEO-10, and EMP-01.

## Codes: one scheme, usable in briefs

Codes were renumbered into a three-letter family prefix and a number, without gaps in a family: GEO, TYP, INT, TIM, SYS, SRV, AGT, EMP, OBJ, MEC, CRF, NAT, plus COS for the two coss.com concepts. The `category` field now names the family, and `familyPrefix` in `components/plotter/study-types.ts` ties them together.

Every coded drawing is bundled in the skill as a resolved static SVG (`skills/plotter/references/catalogue/`, generated by `bun run catalogue`), so a brief can say “like TIM-06” without the website. `plotter:check` fails on a malformed code, a gap, or a catalogue that is out of date.

Older records keep the codes they were written with. `skills/plotter/references/history/code-map.md` maps every former code to its current one, including the retired drawings.
